How it's built

Western Studio is deliberately small. The tools are built by someone who works in education — not a software house removed from it — using modern, AI-assisted development. That combination is the whole point: a real classroom problem becomes a working, tested tool in days rather than budget cycles, and it stays close to how schools actually work.

Lean doesn't mean loose. Where it matters — how staff data is stored, who can see what, how it's secured — the work is done properly: document-level access controls, rules tested before they ship, and a proper data-protection assessment. One person understands every part of it and stands behind it. That isn't a limitation — it's why the tools are practical, quick to improve, and answerable to the people who use them.
